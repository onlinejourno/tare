# Unified crawler User-Agent for all Signal Probes

All Signal Probes — RSS paths, editorial institutional paths, and article HTML fetches — use the same `DemocraticInfrastructureChecker/1.0` User-Agent rather than spoofing a real browser UA. The alternative (using a Chrome UA for article fetches) produces higher signal yield on Cloudflare-protected Publications, but at the cost of identity inconsistency: the tool would present itself honestly on some requests and deceptively on others. We chose consistency: a publisher who wants accurate analysis can allowlist `DemocraticInfrastructureChecker` in their Cloudflare Bot Management rules, and the tool's UA becomes a coordination surface rather than a disguise. The Chrome UA deception is also fragile — bot management fingerprints beyond UA strings and will eventually fail regardless.
